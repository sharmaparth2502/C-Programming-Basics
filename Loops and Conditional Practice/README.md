# Loops and Conditional Practice

This folder contains simple C programs for practicing loops, conditional statements, logical operators, and number-based conditions.

## Programs

1. Print even numbers between 20 and 70
2. Print numbers between 20 and 40 and even numbers between 50 and 80
3. Print even numbers below 50 and odd numbers above 50
4. Print even numbers between 20 and 40 and odd numbers between 50 and 80
5. Print multiples of 3 or 7
6. Print numbers within specified ranges
7. Print even numbers that are not multiples of 3 or 5
8. Print numbers that are multiples of 3 or 5, but not both
9. Print numbers whose last digit is a multiple of 3
10. Print numbers whose last digit is between 5 and 8

## Concepts Used

* `for` loop
* `if-else`
* Modulus operator `%`
* Logical operators
* Number-based conditions
* Basic iteration

These programs are written for learning and practice.
